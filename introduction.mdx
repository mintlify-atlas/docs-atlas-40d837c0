## What is Virtual Catalog?

Virtual Catalog is a full-stack digital commerce solution built with **Flutter 3.x** and **Firebase**. A single deployment supports multiple businesses through slug-based multi-tenancy — each business gets its own branded storefront at `/<business-slug>` with its own products, banners, settings, and order flow.

## Key capabilities

<Steps>
  <Step title="Storefront for customers">
    Customers browse your catalog at `/<your-slug>`, filter by category, price, and size, view rich product galleries, and select color/size variants before adding to cart.
  </Step>
  <Step title="WhatsApp checkout">
    After selecting items, customers fill in a shipping form and send a fully structured order summary to your WhatsApp number — no payment gateway required for basic setups.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Admin panel">
    Log in at `/<your-slug>/admin` to create and edit products with multiple variants, manage promotional banners, configure delivery and payment methods, and customize your storefront theme.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Cloud-native media">
    Product images are automatically compressed to WebP and served globally via Cloudinary — upload once, fast everywhere.
  </Step>
</Steps>
